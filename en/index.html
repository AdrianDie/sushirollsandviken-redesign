Hidden menu

Kakure Omakase

Kakure Omakase is an exclusive, hidden dining experience where guests are served a personal menu composed by our master chef. Every course builds on the best ingredients of the season, carefully selected to bring out natural flavours and clean textures. The meal is served in an intimate, minimalist setting that lets you focus fully on the journey of flavours, the craft and the details that make Japanese gastronomy unique.

The experience follows the omakase tradition, where the chef guides you through a meal tailored to the evening. No two experiences are quite the same, and the combination of technique, creativity and respect for the ingredients creates an atmosphere that is both calm and refined. Kakure Omakase is not just a meal but a deeper exploration of Japanese culinary art, inviting you to taste, learn and discover something new with every course.

About us

Japanese craft in Sandviken

Sushi Roll Sandviken sits in the heart of a quiet, charming street in Bergen, a place where Japanese food traditions meet modern flavours. We are passionate about creating clean, fresh and balanced dishes, with a focus on quality in every single ingredient. Here you will find everything from classic nigiri and sashimi to creative maki rolls and warm small dishes, for new guests and experienced sushi lovers alike.

We want every visit to feel like a small break from everyday life. That is why we care about good ingredients, a pleasant atmosphere and service that makes you feel looked after. Whether you want a relaxed dinner, takeaway or our exclusive Kakure Omakase, our goal is always the same: to give you a flavourful, memorable experience you will want to come back to.
